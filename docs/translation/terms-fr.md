# Terminologie contraignante — Talmud des dix Sefirot → français (`fr`)

Binding terminology for every translator of TES into French. **Bnei Baruch's own French decides**; where BB is silent the row is marked PROPOSED and says which BB pattern it extends. 234 rows: 215 OFFICIAL, 19 PROPOSED. Machine-readable twin: `terms-fr.json` (same rows, plus the `required` / `banned` gate lists).

**Evidence (all official BB French), with the sigla used in every count below:**

- **I** — _Introduction à l'étude des dix Sefirot_ (KabbalahMedia unit OqZMFGHu, 155 numbered sections aligned with the Hebrew).
- **P** — _Préface à la sagesse de la Kabbale_ (Petiha, 187 numbered sections aligned with the Hebrew). BB's technical-vocabulary text.
- **T** — TES partie 6 as imported in this repo (`content/parts/part-06/chapters/*/{source,commentary}.fr-bb.json`: 54 source items, 8 Lumière intérieure items), paired item by item with `he-jerusalem-1956`.

Counts are whole-word Python regex matches over the plain text (typographic apostrophe normalised). "A 225 / B 25" means BB writes A 225 times and B 25 times. Hebrew↔French correspondences were checked section by section (Hebrew section N against French section N), not just by global frequency.

**Read this first.** BB's French has two registers. P and I (the large, BB-France texts) transliterate the Kabbalistic nouns with French phonetics and gloss them once; T (translated via BB's English) calques English (écran, couplage, internalité, Partzouf, Atzilout). P + I outnumber T on almost every term, often by 10:1, so **the binding forms are P + I's**. T's forms are listed as attested alternatives: never "correct" them in the fr-bb chapters, but don't use them in new text.

### 1.2 Transliteration scheme (BB French phonetics)

- **ו (u) → ou**: Partsouf, Gouf, Tabour, Zivoug, Tsimtsoum, Malkhout, Noukva, Katnout, Ibour, Akoudim, Sioum.
- **צ → ts**: Atsilout 225 / Atzilout 25; Partsouf(im) 469 / Partzouf(im) 33; Tsimtsoum 68; Yetsira 66; Mitsvot 75. BB keeps _tz_ only in Metzakh (9) and Etzbaot (2).
- **soft כ → kh**: Malkhout 173, Hokhma 153, Bekhina 314, Massakh 242, Hizdakhout 24, Melekh.
- **ח → H at the start of a word, kh elsewhere**: Hokhma, Hessed, Hassadim, Haya, Hotem, Holam, Hirik — but Rouakh 22, Yekhida 41, Akhoraim 40, Mokhin 106, Rakhamim 9, Metzakh 9, Netsakh. One word-initial exception BB fixes: Khazé 67 (Hazeh 10, T only).
- **ש → ch or sh, by word** (follow the table): Roch, Rechimo, Kachiout, Bitouch, Hitlabchout, Kedoucha _(ch)_; Shoresh, Shourouk, Shibbolet, Nefesh, Neshama _(sh)_.
- **ס between vowels → ss**: Hessed, Yessod, Massakh, Assiya. **ג before e/i → gu**: Guevoura, Guimel, Guématrie, Taguin.
- **Final ה is dropped**: Hokhma, Bina (129; Binah 62 is T only), Yetsira, Assiya, Haya, Noukva, Bekhina, Sefira, Parsa. Exceptions: פה → Pé, חזה → Khazé.
- **No apostrophe for ע / א**: Taamim 30 / Ta'amim 1, ZA 69 / Z'A 50, YESHOUT 34 / YISH'SOUT 18, Daat, Akoudim.
- **Hebrew article in names**: joined _Ha_ + capital — Baal HaSoulam, Shibbolet HaZakan, Shaar HaHakdamot, Sefer HaIlan. BB's own spaced forms stay as BB prints them: Midat ha Din, Melekh ha Daat, Mador haKlipot.

### 1.3 Capitalisation, acronyms, elision, gender

- Transliterated technical nouns are capitalised (Kli, Massakh, Aviout, Partsouf, Roch, Sefirot …). Translated words stay lower case: lumière 377 / Lumière 149, monde 481 / Monde 10, degré, niveau, phase, correction. BB's fixed capitals: **la Lumière Supérieure**, **le Supérieur** (45 / 21) but **l'inférieur**, **le Créateur**, **l'Emanateur** (no accent on the capital: 20 / Émanateur 0).
- **Acronyms** are Latin capitals with no dots or hyphens, exactly as in English: AK, AB, SAG, MA, BON, KHB, HGT, NHY, NHYM, HB, HBD, KHBD, TNHY, ZAT, GAR, VAK, GE, AHP, ZA, AA, AVI, ZON, YESHOUT, ABYA, BYA, NRNHY, NRN, MAN, TANTA, HaVaYaH. AVI, ZON, GAR and ZAT take **plural verbs** ("AVI sont" 13 / 5, "ZON sont" 12 / 1). Expand only where the Hebrew spells the name out (Arikh Anpin, Zeir Anpin, Adam Kadmon, Galgalta ve Eynaim, Abba et Ima); otherwise give BB's gloss once per item: ABYA (Atsilout, Briya, Yetsira, Assiya), AVI (Abba et Ima), ZAT (sept inférieures), GAR [trois premières Sefirot], Ozen, Hotem, Pé [AHP], MAN [Mayin Noukvin].
- **No elision before a transliterated name or acronym**: _de AK_ 356 / _d'AK_ 0; _de Atsilout_ 189 / 3; _de AHP_ 28 / 0; _de Akhoraim_ 24 / 0; _le ARI, du ARI, au ARI_ 21 / _l'ARI_ 0. Prefer _de Ein Sof_ (13 / 6). The definite article does elide before transliterated common nouns: _l'Aviout_ (90), _l'Hizdakhout_, _l'Hitkalelout_.
- **Gender** (BB's agreement): masculine _le Massakh_ (225), _le Partsouf_ (190), _le Zivoug_ (71), _le Tsimtsoum_ (49), _le Rechimo_ (29), _le Kli_ (102), _le Gouf_ (59), _le Tabour_ (107), _le Khazé_ (27), _le Hey_ (22 / _la Hey_ 0); feminine _la Sefira_ (34), _la Bekhina_ (19), _la Parsa_ (66 / 1), _la Malkhout_, _l'Aviout_, and every named light, like _lumière_: _la / cette Ohr Hozer_ (8 / 0). Plurals: Kélim, Partsoufim, Rochim, Goufim, Bekhinot, Rechimot, Zivouguim, Klipot (sg. Klipa), Sefirot (sg. Sefira).

### 1.4 Glosses, quotation marks, typography

- **First-use gloss**: the transliteration plus BB's gloss **in square brackets** (BB: 211 bracketed glosses / 77 parenthetical). Use BB's wording, given in bold in the note column of the table (Massakh [écran], Roch [tête], Tabour [nombril], Katnout [petitesse] …). Plurals get BB's "(pluriel de Partsouf)". After the first use in an item, use the bare form.
- **Quotation marks**: « … » with a no-break space (U+00A0) inside (BB's HTML: 249 + 78 NBSP after «). Never straight " (I + P: 525 guillemet pairs, 0 straight quotes; T's 68 straight quotes come from its import path). Inner quotation ‘…’ (6, against 3 nested « »). Apostrophe ’ (I + P: 3,711 / ' 2). French spacing: a space before : ; ? ! as BB does.
- **Italics**: BB's Préface italicises every transliteration (5,138 `<em>`), but the TES pane does not (T: 32 `<em>`, none systematic). Do **not** add `<em>`; carry over only the markup that is in the Hebrew item.
- **Address the reader as vous**: Sachez 28, vous savez 20, Souvenez-vous 6, against 2 tu-forms. ותדע → Sachez; כבר ידעת → vous savez déjà; והבן → et comprenez cela.
- **Numbers**: Arabic numerals in citations (page 297, point 35, Partie 5). The four named phases keep their Hebrew letter names (Bekhina Aleph … Dalet); counted Sefirot are words (les dix Sefirot, les trois premières).

### 1.5 The bold lemma quoting the Ari (commentary panes)

Keep the source's `<b>…</b>` exactly where the Hebrew has it. Inside it, use **the wording of the French Ari pane for that line**: the translated source layer the reader sees beside the note. Write it as plain bold, with no guillemets and no italics. `וכו'` inside the lemma becomes _etc._ (BB T: « Il examine ces lumières de AHP, etc. »). Keep the Hebrew's colon after the closing tag, French-spaced: `<b>…</b> : …`. (BB's own fr-bb items end the lemma with a full stop and let the bold run on, which is an import artefact. Don't copy it.)

### 1.6 Markers and citations in one paragraph

Inline Ohr Pnimi markers `(כג)` → `(23)` (gematria, Arabic numerals), never the Hebrew letter. BB deletes them in its own edition, but the reader aligns notes by them. Item → _point_ (BB: « (Point 35) » 113, mid-sentence « au point 15 »: the first word of a parenthetical reference is capitalised, « (Partie 5, Lumière intérieure, point 40) »). Page → _page N_ (folio side: _page 6b_). Part → _partie N_, chapter → _chapitre N_, Etz Chaim gate → _porte N_. BB French **drops** ד״ה, עש״ה and ע״ש entirely. Our pipeline keeps them, so use the PROPOSED forms in §2.9. Never drop a citation because BB would.

### 3.4 Conflicts with the repo, flagged and not silently resolved

1. **Histaklut Pnimit: « Regard intérieur » (BB) vs « Observation intérieure » (app).** BB's only French name for the commentary is _Histaklout Pnimit [Regard intérieur]_ (Intro §156), and BB renders הסתכלות as _regard_: none of BB's 3 « observation » renders it (all three are « l'observation de la Torah »). The app writes « Observation intérieure » in **20 keys of `i18n/locales/fr.json`** (reader.pane.innerObservation, reader.commentarySection.histaklut-pnimit, …) and **100 French ToC titles** (« Observation intérieure N »). This table binds **Regard intérieur** for running text, per the official-first rule. If the owner keeps the UI wording, swap it in the outputs with one global replace (the string is unique). Either way the UI and the translation should end up saying the same thing.
2. **Q&A headings.** BB calls פירוש המלות _l'explication des mots_ and ענינים _sujets_ (Intro §156: « un tableau de questions et réponses pour tous les mots et sujets »). The ToC's 128 titles say « … sur le sens des mots / sur les thèmes » (0 in BB). Translators rarely write these headings; the table gives BB's words.
3. **« du Ari » (app, 14 strings) vs « du ARI » (BB 21 / 2).** Running text follows BB: **le ARI**.
4. **The fr-bb chapters of part 6 use T's register** (Atzilout, Partzouf, Binah, Rosh, Peh, Hazeh, Siyoum, Einayim, Nekoudim, Z'A, AvI, YISH'SOUT, kli/kelim, écran, couplage, internalité, goûts, phase quatre). They are official and stay as they are. A reader will see two registers side by side in part 6. That is a property of BB's own texts, not something translators should "fix".

### 3.5 The decisions I am least sure of

1. **Regard intérieur** over the app's _Observation intérieure_ (BB evidence is one gloss; the app has 120 strings the other way).
2. **Generic בחינה → phase** (159) over _discernement_ (80) and _Bekhinat X_ (23). The English run chose "discernment" for the same construction; French BB's P + I lean _discernement_ for countable "N בחינות" (35 + 33 ordinals vs phases 33 + 10), while T's _la phase de X_ (96) carries the total.
3. **Netsakh** against the raw majority _Netzah_ 9 / 6 (scheme ruling).
4. **Daat** against _Da'at_ 21 / 20 (scheme ruling).
5. **Nefesh / Neshama** decided as a pair (sh 53 / 29), overriding _Nefech_ 16 / 8 on its own count.
6. **Ohr Hokhma** (12 / 13) and **Ohr Yashar** (2 / 3) transliterated by the family rule against their own per-term counts.
7. **Proposed citation formulae**: _le passage commençant par « … »_ (ד״ה), _étudiez-le bien là-bas_ (עש״ה), _voir là-bas_ (ע״ש), _fin de citation_ (עכ״ל). BB omits all four, so there is no official model.
8. **Abba** (8 / 6, strict majority) although BB's own AVI gloss is _(Aba et Ima)_ and the English canon is _Aba_.
9. **expansion** for התפשטות (single most frequent form) while the two transliterated spellings together (11) outnumber it.
10. **cercles / droiture** for עיגולים / יושר: no evidence at all (PROPOSED). _droiture_ is the riskiest coinage in the table.
